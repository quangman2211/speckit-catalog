# Cong a11y — WCAG 2.1 AA

> Sao chep va sua doi tu skill `accessibility-review` cua
> [anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins](https://github.com/anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins),
> Apache-2.0. **Da sua:** doi nguon vao tu Figma sang spec/plan/contracts, them
> lop ghi file, them luat FAIL-hay-GAP, bo phan connector.

Soat truoc khi viet code, khong phai sau. Sua mot thieu sot a11y luc con la chu
trong spec re hon nhieu lan sua khi da thanh component.

## Giai doan

**PLAN**, chay truoc `__SPECKIT_COMMAND_IMPLEMENT__`.

## User Input

$ARGUMENTS

De trong = soat moi man hinh cua lat hien tai.

## Steps

1. Doc `.specify/extensions/phases/phases-config.yml` lay `ui_contract`
   (mac dinh `contracts/ui.md`) va `wcag_level` (mac dinh `AA`).

2. Doc `spec.md`, `plan.md` cua lat hien tai, va `<ui_contract>` neu co.
   Khong co hop dong giao dien thi noi ro trong bao cao — phan lon tieu chi se
   ra GAP chu khong ra PASS, va do la thong tin dung.

3. Doi chieu tung tieu chi ap dung duoc (bang tham chieu o duoi).

4. Ghi ket qua vao `specs/<lat>/a11y-review.md`.

5. Co muc **FAIL** thi dung lai va bao nguoi dung sua spec hoac hop dong giao
   dien truoc khi implement. Chi co GAP thi duoc di tiep, nhung phai liet ke ra.

## Luat quan trong nhat: FAIL hay GAP

- **FAIL** — spec hoac hop dong **noi ro** mot dieu vi pham tieu chi.
  Vi du: hop dong ghi mau chu `#9aa` tren nen `#fff` (ty le 2.1:1).
- **GAP** — spec **im lang** ve tieu chi do.
  Vi du: khong cho nao noi thu tu focus.

Bao GAP thanh FAIL lam nguoi ta mat long tin vao cong nay roi bo qua no. Bao FAIL
thanh GAP lam cong nay vo dung. Phan biet cho dung.

## WCAG 2.1 AA — tham chieu nhanh

### Cam nhan duoc
- **1.1.1** Noi dung khong phai chu co alt text
- **1.3.1** Thong tin va cau truc truyen dat bang ngu nghia, khong chi bang hinh
- **1.4.3** Tuong phan >= 4.5:1 (chu thuong), >= 3:1 (chu lon)
- **1.4.11** Tuong phan phi-chu >= 3:1 (component giao dien, do hoa)

### Thao tac duoc
- **2.1.1** Moi chuc nang dung duoc bang ban phim
- **2.4.3** Thu tu focus hop ly
- **2.4.7** Chi bao focus nhin thay duoc
- **2.5.5** Vung cham >= 44x44 CSS px

### Hieu duoc
- **3.2.1** Focus vao khong gay thay doi bat ngo
- **3.3.1** Loi duoc chi ra va mo ta duoc
- **3.3.2** Moi o nhap co nhan hoac huong dan

### Vung chai
- **4.1.2** Moi component co name, role, value

## Tam loi hay gap

1. Tuong phan mau khong du
2. Thieu nhan cho o nhap
3. Phan tu tuong tac khong den duoc bang ban phim
4. Anh mang thong tin ma thieu alt
5. Bay focus trong modal
6. Thieu ARIA landmark
7. Media tu chay ma khong co nut dieu khien
8. Gioi han thoi gian ma khong cho gia han

## Rules

- **Chi bao FAIL khi spec noi ro dieu vi pham.** Spec im lang thi bao GAP.
- **Khong tu sua spec hay hop dong.** Lenh nay bao cao va de xuat, khong ghi de
  quyet dinh cua nguoi khac.
- **Khong doc code de soat.** Cong nay chay truoc khi co code. Soat code la viec
  cua `__SPECKIT_COMMAND_ANALYZE__` va review.
- Tuong phan tinh tu gia tri that trong `design-tokens.json` neu co. Khong co
  file do thi moi tieu chi mau deu la GAP — noi ro ly do, dung doan.
